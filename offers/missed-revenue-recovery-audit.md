# Missed Revenue Recovery Audit

## Offer

**For independent plumbing and HVAC companies**

You may not need more leads. You may need to stop losing the leads you already paid for.

CashGPT's Missed Revenue Recovery Audit maps what happens after an unanswered phone call or web inquiry, estimates the economics of the current leakage, and produces a concrete recovery workflow.

**Price: $149 fixed.** If implementation is justified and purchased, the $149 is credited toward implementation.

We do not promise recovered revenue before examining the business's own numbers. If the economics do not justify a change, the report says so.

## Five-question qualification

1. Roughly how many inbound calls and web leads do you receive in a normal month?
2. About what percentage of calls go unanswered, hit voicemail, or arrive after hours?
3. What is a typical booked job worth in revenue, and approximately what gross margin do you retain?
4. What currently happens after a missed call or unanswered web inquiry, and how quickly?
5. What CRM, field-service, calendar, or phone systems do you currently use (for example ServiceTitan, Jobber, Housecall Pro, GoHighLevel, Google Calendar, or none)?

## Audit calculator

Use client-provided numbers. Mark estimates explicitly.

- `monthly_inbound` = monthly inbound calls/leads
- `unanswered_rate` = fraction not handled live or promptly
- `unanswered_leads = monthly_inbound * unanswered_rate`
- `job_revenue` = average booked-job revenue
- `gross_margin` = gross profit fraction
- `baseline_booking_rate` = booking rate for handled qualified leads
- `recoverable_fraction` = conservative fraction of unanswered leads that a better workflow could re-engage
- `recovered_bookings = unanswered_leads * recoverable_fraction * baseline_booking_rate`
- `recovered_revenue = recovered_bookings * job_revenue`
- `recovered_gross_profit = recovered_revenue * gross_margin`
- `ninety_day_recovered_gp = recovered_gross_profit * 3`

Do not present the output as guaranteed revenue. It is a scenario estimate based on supplied assumptions.

## Decision rule

Implementation is worth quoting only when the conservative 90-day recovered gross-profit estimate supports at least **5x client ROI** relative to the proposed implementation fee.

`implementation_fee_ceiling = ninety_day_recovered_gp / 5`

If the result is too small, recommend no implementation or only a cheaper operational fix.

## One-page client report

### 1. Current leak
- Monthly inbound volume
- Estimated unanswered/slow-response volume
- Current callback/follow-up process
- Primary failure points

### 2. Conservative economics
- Estimated recoverable opportunities/month
- Estimated recovered bookings/month
- Estimated recovered revenue/month
- Estimated recovered gross profit/month
- Assumptions and uncertainty

### 3. Recovery workflow
Map only what is needed:

`capture -> immediate acknowledgement -> qualification -> routing/pipeline -> booking -> follow-up -> reactivation -> measurement`

Possible components include missed-call text-back, after-hours intake, human callback routing, booking links, CRM updates, quote follow-up, dormant-lead reactivation, and reporting. Do not prescribe AI voice or paid software unless it materially improves the economics.

### 4. Recommendation
Choose one:
- **No change justified** — current leakage does not support intervention.
- **Operational fix** — process/script/routing change is sufficient.
- **Automation justified** — provide a scoped implementation quote whose fee remains below the 5x-ROI ceiling.
