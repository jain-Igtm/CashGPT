# TOPx HHS Tech Sprint for AI and Invisible Illness

## Why this is on the board

This is a current U.S. HHS/NIH-backed prize competition with up to **$2,000,000 in total cash prizes**, including a reported **$1,000,000 grand prize**. Phase 2 is open from **July 29, 2026 through October 15, 2026 at 11:59 PM ET** according to the NIH challenge page surfaced in current research.

This is exactly the kind of non-generic, rule-created opportunity CashGPT should investigate: a large prize pool, open-data requirement, AI allowed/encouraged, and a long enough submission window to build a differentiated technical artifact rather than cold-sell another commodity service.

## Verified public facts

- HHS announced the TOPx HHS Tech Sprint for AI and Invisible Illness as part of its Lyme disease / invisible illness innovation program.
- HHS describes the sprint as a national prize competition using U.S. Open Data and AI.
- HHS states that the program offers up to $2,000,000 in total prizes; an HHS press release states the program includes a $1,000,000 grand prize.
- NIH's current challenge page reports Phase 2 open 07/29/26 to 10/15/26 11:59 PM ET.
- HHS frames the opportunity around building digital tools and AI-accelerated insights for Lyme disease, invisible illness, and cost of illness using HHS Open Data.

## First technical thesis to pressure-test

Build a public-data "diagnostic delay / care-access map" that combines HHS/CDC open datasets with geography and provider-access indicators to identify counties or populations with unusually high mismatch between likely invisible-illness burden and available diagnostic/care infrastructure. The edge would be the **data synthesis + prioritization layer**, not a generic symptom chatbot.

Potential outputs:
- county-level risk / access score;
- explainable factor decomposition;
- patient/provider navigation layer;
- cost-of-delay estimator;
- reproducible open-data pipeline;
- evidence packet showing why the tool surfaces areas existing systems miss.

This thesis is provisional until the official rules and scoring rubric are fully verified.

## Kill / pursue rule

**Pursue** only if: new entrants are still eligible, AI-generated/AI-assisted development is allowed, no expensive credential or institutional requirement blocks Jane/CashGPT, submission requirements can be met with available tools, and expected prize EV plausibly dominates the team's current commodity-service funnel.

**Kill** immediately if: Phase 2 is closed to new entrants, eligibility requires an institution/credential we do not have, or rules make a compliant submission impossible with our available toolchain.
