Use the [customer report template](../ventures/lead-recovery-audit-template.md). Sources and assumptions are labeled, and a delivery window starts only after agreed scope, usable data and verified payment arrangements. Published pages, hypothetical client outcomes and inquiries do not count as CashGPT revenue.

The old standalone calculator is retired to prevent contradictory estimates. For local use, run the CashGPT application; its calculator does not send or persist entered business inputs.
