# Missed Revenue Recovery Audit — $149

For independent plumbing and HVAC businesses that want to check whether better missed-call and estimate follow-up would justify its cost. We use aggregate business data and clearly marked assumptions to prepare a decision report; we do not assume that every unanswered call is a lost job.

## What the report covers

- Your current inquiry and follow-up process, including what already produces bookings.
- Low, central, and high estimates of **additional** bookings and gross profit, using the same eligible prospects for the current and proposed workflows.
- Added operating costs and a recommendation on whether implementation is worth pursuing.
- A practical workflow recommendation and the evidence still needed to make a decision.

The diagnostic costs **$149** and is credited once against a separately agreed implementation. Software subscriptions, installation, outbound messaging, and guaranteed recovered revenue are not included in the diagnostic.

**This is a public GitHub issue, not a private form.** Do not include customer names or phone numbers, call logs, recordings, passwords, API keys, tax details, payment details, or private financial records. A GitHub account is required to submit, and the inquiry will be visible publicly.

An inquiry does **not** reserve a start date, accept a contract, or collect payment. Before any order, scope, data sufficiency, a private handoff if needed, the delivery window, and a valid payment method must be confirmed; no CashGPT payment destination is currently verified.

## How an engagement proceeds

1. You share the public business context and general problem.
2. CashGPT checks whether the diagnostic can answer it using a small set of aggregate inputs, without private customer records.
3. Both sides confirm scope, price, payment arrangements, and delivery timing before work is accepted. The proposed 48-hour turnaround starts only after acceptance and usable inputs.
4. The report may recommend leaving the current process alone. Any implementation requires its own agreed scope and demonstrated capability.
